Format

You will play a series of 8 rounds by September 1st.

Each round you will keep your net score with Stableford points.

Submit all scorecards to JZ in person or throw them in his locker.

Scorecard

Please keep track of your gross score on your scorecard and delineate which holes you receive a stroke on. Below your score for each hole, indicate how many Stableford points you earned for that hole, and add everything up at the end.

Stableford Scoring

Net Double Bogey or worse0 pts
Net Bogey1 pt
Net Par2 pts
Net Birdie3 pts
Net Eagle4 pts
Net Albatross5 pts
Example: If you got a 5 on Hole 3 (bogey) but you get a stroke, write down the 5 and delineate in the box with a dot or * that there is a stroke for the hole. Because it is net par, write down 2 points below your score for that hole. Add up your Stableford points at the end of the round.

The top 6 with the most total points after 8 rounds make the playoffs. Seeds #1 and #2 receive first-round byes. First round matchups: #3 vs #6 and #4 vs #5. Semifinals: winner of #4 vs #5 plays #1; winner of #3 vs #6 plays #2.

Rules

  • All play from the Blue tees
  • Current handicap in effect the day of play (plug into GHIN)
  • You must text the group the day you plan to play
  • You must play with at least 1 member of the group to validate scores
  • Lift, clean, and place in effect for all fairways when they are over watered — check with your playing partner
