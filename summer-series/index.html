Format

You will play a series of 8 rounds by September 1st.

Each round you will keep your net score with Stableford points.

Stableford Scoring

Net Double Bogey or worse0 pts
Net Bogey1 pt
Net Par2 pts
Net Birdie3 pts
Net Eagle4 pts
Net Albatross5 pts
Example: You get a 5 on Hole 3, but your handicap gives you a stroke — that is net par, so you earn 2 points for that hole. Add up your points at the end of each round.

The top 6 with the most total points after 8 rounds make the playoffs. Seeds #1 and #2 receive first-round byes. First round matchups: #3 vs #6 and #4 vs #5. Semifinals: winner of #4 vs #5 plays #1; winner of #3 vs #6 plays #2.

Rules

  • All play from the Blue tees
  • Current handicap in effect the day of play (plug into GHIN)
  • You must text the group the day you plan to play
  • You must play with at least 1 member of the group to validate scores
  • Lift, clean, and place in effect for all fairways when they are over watered — check with your playing partner
